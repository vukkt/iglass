# Contrast report

_Generated by `tools/build_palettes.py`. Do not edit by hand._

Each color is checked against the window background blended over the desktop behind it:

- **Tinted, Solid**: over `#000000` and `#FFFFFF`
- **Frosted**: over `#333333` and `#CCCCCC`
- **Clear**: over `#808080`

Tinted and Solid hold up over any wallpaper. Frosted is checked over dark and light grey, standing in for typical blurred wallpapers. Clear is an aesthetic choice; at its translucency no palette can stay readable over every wallpaper.

The **contrast** column is the worst case across those backgrounds (WCAG ratio). Base colors are Apple system colors; **adjusted** rows had their lightness nudged to meet the target.

Custom `--opacity` values below **0.66** can drop under these targets over typical wallpapers; iglass warns and asks for `--force`.

The **high contrast** palette (`--contrast high`) follows Apple's advice to give custom colors an increased-contrast option. It uses the same backgrounds with stricter targets.
