Liquid Glass
for your terminal.

A free, open-source theme for iTerm2 and Ghostty, on macOS and Linux. Translucent, built from Apple's system colors, and contrast-checked so it stays readable.

❯brew install vukkt/tap/iglass macOS · Linux
❯curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vukkt/iglass/main/install.sh | bash

Then run iglass install. Undo everything with iglass uninstall.

Glass, done carefully.

Only the look changes. Your font, shortcuts and shell stay yours, and everything iglass touches can be restored exactly.

◐

Real Liquid Glass

In Ghostty on macOS 26+, iglass uses Apple's own glass material, not an imitation.

▤

Four levels of glass

Clear, Frosted, Tinted and Solid, mirroring Apple's options. Pick how much shows through.

●

Eight accents

The macOS accent colors, for your cursor, selection and links.

☾

Light and dark

Separate palettes that follow your system, or stay dark with --appearance dark.

✓

Contrast-checked

Every color is checked against the window as it looks over your desktop, not a flat background.

↺

Safe and reversible

Validated before it's written, backed up first, and undone byte for byte on uninstall.

Pick your glass.

The same palette at four levels of transparency. In Ghostty on macOS, real Liquid Glass.

macOS and Linux.

One command sets up the terminal you're in.

macOS

  • Ghostty · real Liquid Glass on macOS 26+
  • iTerm2 · transparency, blur and a tuned palette
  • Optional native title bar and default profile for iTerm2

Linux

  • Ghostty · blur on KDE Plasma and Hyprland
  • Tinted by default on desktops without blur, like GNOME
